# Privacy Policy for US Calendar

**Last updated:** January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how **US Calendar** ("the App") handles information when you use our calendar application.

## 1. Overview

US Calendar is a calendar app that shows United States public holidays and lets you add personal notes and reminders. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App uses and where it is stored.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device (Local Only)

The following data is stored **only on your device** and is **not** sent to our servers or any third party (except as noted in the Advertising section below):

- **Personal notes:** Any notes you add to dates are saved locally on your device using your device’s local storage. We do not have access to this data.
- **Notification preferences:** Your reminder settings (e.g., how many days before an event to be notified, reminder time) are stored locally.
- **App preferences:** Your chosen state/region for holidays, theme (light/dark), and similar in-app settings are stored locally.

We do **not** collect, transmit, or store your notes or preferences on our own servers.

## 3. Notifications

The App uses **local notifications** to remind you of notes and holidays. Scheduling and delivery of these reminders are handled entirely on your device. No reminder content or schedule is sent to us or to any external service for notification delivery.

## 4. Advertising

The App may show advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. When ads are displayed:

- **Google** may collect and process data (such as device identifiers, advertising ID, and general usage data) in accordance with [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google’s advertising policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).
- We do **not** control what data Google collects. For details and choices (e.g., opt out of personalized ads), see Google’s policies and your device’s ad settings.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. Any data used for ad serving is governed by Google’s policies.

## 5. Holiday and Calendar Data

Holiday and calendar data (e.g., US public holidays) is included in the App and can be used **offline**. We do not send your device location or identity to load this data.

## 6. No Account or Login

The App does **not** require you to create an account or log in. We do not collect names, emails, or other account information.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- **On your device:** Notes and preferences remain on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall the App.
- **To delete your data:** Clear the App’s data (e.g., in your device’s Application/Storage settings for “US Calendar”) or uninstall the App. This will remove all notes and local settings.
- We do not retain copies of your notes or preferences once the App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will work to delete it from our systems. Because we do not collect personal information through the App (notes and preferences are local only), we typically would not have such data to delete.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
